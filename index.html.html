Our story

Cooked with intent, served without pretense.

Lion Kitchen opened on a quiet corner in San Francisco with one idea: food should have a point of view. Our wood-fired oven runs all day, our sauces take three, and nothing leaves the pass without someone tasting it first.

We work with small farms up and down the coast, change the menu with the seasons, and keep the room loud in the best way — full tables, open kitchen, no white tablecloths.
